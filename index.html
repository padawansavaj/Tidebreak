TIDEBREAK

Shipwrecked on an isolated tropical island. Search the shore, gather supplies, manage hunger and thirst, and build your first survival tools.

iPhone: left thumb moves · drag the right side to look (horizontal inverted) · PICK UP collects nearby items. Xbox controller: sticks move/look · A jumps · X collects · Y crafts · B opens bag.
